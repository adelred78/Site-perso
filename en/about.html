About

First-year Bachelor's student in Computer Science at EPSI Paris. What I love: understanding systems, breaking things to fix them, and shipping projects that work.

My background

I earned my mathematics baccalauréat with high honors (16.28/20) in July 2025 at Lycée Amara Rachid in Algiers. In parallel, I had already started coding: an intensive web development bootcamp at Code213 (Algiers), 192 hours of HTML, CSS and JavaScript basics. That's when I knew this was what I wanted to do.

I joined EPSI Paris in 2025 for my Bachelor's in Computer Science. Today, I'm doing a Full Stack internship at Glikt in Courbevoie.

Why computer science

What draws me isn't just writing code. It's understanding how everything holds together. From the TCP packet traveling, to the SQL query responding, to the CSS aligning a button — everything is connected. I love that full-stack dimension.

At EPSI, I didn't want to specialize too early. I'm aiming for excellence across three axes: web, embedded systems and AI. I'll dive deeper later. For now, I'm building on all three.

How I work

I learn by building. Theory in the morning, code in the afternoon. When something breaks, I don't work around it — I want to know why. That's usually where I learn the most.

In teams, I like concrete roles. On the Rube Goldberg project, I was in charge of the Tinkercad design. We won the contest.
